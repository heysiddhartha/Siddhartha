THE IDEA

Don't search harder. Filter better.

RADAR is being built as a discovery layer rather than another giant job database. It normalises public feeds, removes duplicates and ranks opportunities against practical signals such as category relevance, entry-level fit, India location and recency.

The goal: fewer tabs, fewer dead listings, and a faster path from “maybe” to “apply”.
